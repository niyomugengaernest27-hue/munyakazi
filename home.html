GET TO KNOW US

About Intore Learning Center

Intore Learning Center (ILC) is located at Agahozo-Shalom Youth Village in Rubona, Rwamagana District, Eastern Province, Rwanda. It provides a space for learning, training, discussions and activities that support youth development.

The center is associated with activities that help young people develop practical skills, explore entrepreneurship, prepare for employment and discover their creative talents.

GET TO KNOW US

More than a place to stay. A community with purpose.

Intore Learning Community (ILC) is a hospitality and learning destination located within Agahozo-Shalom Youth Village (ASYV), in Rwamagana District, Rwanda.

ILC brings together accommodation, meetings, workshops, retreats and opportunities for people to learn and connect.

It welcomes individuals, organizations, educators, students and groups looking for a meaningful environment in which to stay, meet and learn.

Its connection to ASYV gives the community a wider purpose rooted in education, human connection and positive social impact.

OUR BACKGROUND

OUR STORY

Rooted in a community. Built for connection.

To understand Intore Learning Community, it is important to understand the environment in which it operates.

Agahozo-Shalom Youth Village is a community dedicated to empowering orphaned and vulnerable young people through healing, education and love.

ILC operates within this wider community as a social enterprise. Through hospitality and learning experiences, it connects visitors with an environment that values education, dignity and shared growth.
